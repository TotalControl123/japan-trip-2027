On the day

Gatwick
Be there by 07:40. Bags go straight through to Narita.
Narita
Allow ~1 hour for immigration (Visit Japan Web QR codes ready). Narita Express to Shinjuku, ~80 min, ~¥3,250, reserved seats at the JR desk in arrivals.
Kansai
Be there by 13:30. Rapi:t from Namba (38 min), so leave by 12:45. Extra time is for the tax-free refund.
Shanghai
Both changes are on one ticket. You don't pass immigration; there's a transfer security check, so keep liquids under 100ml. Download maps and films before you go, because Google and WhatsApp don't work on Chinese wifi.
Fare rules
Non-refundable. Date changes possible: up to £150 per ticket plus any fare difference.
